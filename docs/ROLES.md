# What each school profile sees and does

The rule: each profile sees what is part of their job. Supervising is not the same as doing the work: the Dirección checks that attendance was taken, but does not take it.

| Area | Dirección (supervises) | Secretaría (office and fees) | Docente (their sections) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Panel** | **Requiere su atención** (exceptions only): families waiting more than a day for a reply, enrollment applications not yet contacted, payment receipts unreviewed for more than 2 days, sections without attendance after 9:00 (with "Recordar"). **La escuela hoy**: enrollment for next year, fees collected this month, attendance in one line, notice reading. The week and overdue fees | Pending tasks: receipts to review, new applications, office messages, absences reported by families (the calls the front desk takes), overdue fees | **Mi día**: take attendance and send the daily report in one tap, messages, homework, progress of grades |
| Asistencia | Read every section; can record it when covering for a teacher | Not available | Take attendance for their sections |
| Vida diaria (daily report, photos) | Supervise; can fill it in when covering for a teacher | Not available | Fill in and send |
| Académico (grades, home reports, homework, report cards) | Supervise and publish report cards; can fill in when covering | Not available | Fill in grades, reports and homework |
| Mensajes | All conversations | Office conversations | Their sections |
| Avisos | Whole school | Whole school | Their sections |
| Alumnos | Everyone; edits records | Everyone; edits records | Their sections; read only |
| Calendario | Whole school | Whole school | Whole school and their sections |
| Cobros | Yes | Yes | No |
| Matrícula | Yes | Yes | No |
| Reportes (Excel) | All | All (including the monthly attendance report for MEP paperwork) | Attendance for their sections |
| Configuración | All of it | Only "Pagos y precios" (fees, bank account) | No |

## Families

Families see only their own children: home screen (what needs attention, today in class, homework, photos), notices and permission requests, messages to the teacher and the office, calendar, account statement and payment reporting, child profile, report cards and progress reports once published, and their profile.

## How it is enforced

The menu shows each profile only its own areas, and every page and server action checks the role on the server. Hiding a link is not enough, so typing the address directly does not grant access.
